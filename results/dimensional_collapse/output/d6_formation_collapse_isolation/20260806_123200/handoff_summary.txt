DSD D6 formation-collapse-stability/isolation handoff
Author: Kwon Dominicus
Status: Construction / Consistency Check

Prestructure
- N-dimensional candidate/exchange regime with a finite normalized energy reservoir
- not a D7 bulk, material boundary, or infinite-energy space

New state measurements
- formation and collapse events
- local versus connected Gram rank
- prestructure inflow, high-rank outflow, and net import
- collapse release ledger
- scale factor, H, volume factor, accelerated interval, and e-fold
- particle and rank expansion-speed ratios
- connected-to-isolated energy transfer
- stable, relaxing, and extinct terminal states

Baseline results
- balanced: stable D3, 1.339811 e-fold, particle isolation 0.326549, rank-energy isolation 0.957443
- strong expansion: relaxing D3, 1.653648 e-fold, particle isolation 0.712407, rank-energy isolation 0.990774
- extinction: formed episode below threshold at 8.344 tau, final episode energy 0.000434
- retained high-rank: stable local/connected D5, 0.116560 e-fold, negligible isolation

Critical finding
- in the strong-expansion case connected D4->D3 occurred at 4.516 tau while local D4->D3 occurred at 5.314 tau
- expansion-driven isolation can lower connected rank before local rank disappears

Grid
- 125 cells
- 36 cells ended with local rank greater than connected rank
- e-fold range 0.338382 to 1.689409
- no inflation reproduction; values are far below 60 e-fold

Limits
- phenomenological normalized projection, not Friedmann dynamics
- isolation thresholds and coupling coefficients are construction parameters
